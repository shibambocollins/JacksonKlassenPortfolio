Police Briefing on Taxi Violence in the Western Cape

Green Point Police Station · 29 October 2026

A police briefing on taxi violence in the Western Cape is underway at the Green Point Police Station. Provincial Police Commissioner Lieutenant General Sizakele Dyanti highlighted ongoing violence and shootings involving the taxi industry. He also confirmed that operations supported by crime intelligence are ready to address the situation.

Commissioner for the City of Cape Town's Safety and Security Directorate, Johan Brandt, says law enforcement will do everything possible to stop the violence. He confirmed that arrests have been made in connection with the incidents since 16 October and that the cases were proceeding in court.

General Dyanti also addressed deployment at taxi ranks, especially in Delft, and preparations for the upcoming elections. He added that deployment plans were in place, with support from the City of Cape Town. Additional measures, including drones and cameras, are also part of the operational plan to improve public safety.

The Inquest into the Death of Anele Tembe

Cape Town Magistrate's Court · October 2026

The inquest into the death of 22-year-old Anele Tembe, who was engaged to the late rapper Kiernan "AKA" Forbes, is examining forensic evidence surrounding her fatal fall from a hotel balcony in April 2021.

Proceedings have focused on crime scene evidence, footprints recovered from the balcony and scenario tests conducted to examine possibilities regarding the fall. Lieutenant Colonel Jeremy Philander, a forensic analyst and ballistic engineer, testified about simulations using a dummy. His evidence was challenged in cross-examination by Tembe's legal representative, with questions raised about the tests, the calculations and the limitations of the reconstruction.

The court also heard testimony about photographs taken at the scene and the collection of forensic evidence from the hotel room and balcony. At least seven witnesses were expected to testify during the week.

Tuesday, 20 October 2026

A witness was cross-examined about the test dummy scenarios, including calculations relating to the speed and point of impact, and whether the evidence could determine if Tembe was pushed or jumped. The witness said being pushed was a possibility but that there was not enough evidence to confirm it. The court also discussed the post-mortem findings, possible signs of a struggle, and injuries reportedly observed on Forbes.

Testimony covered substances found in Tembe's blood, which the witness said were in quantities too low to be measured. Dr Belinga Bongwalanga's planned testimony via Microsoft Teams was delayed by technical difficulties. The witness confirmed that no tests or samples were taken from Forbes for drugs or alcohol.

The proceedings highlighted the importance of examining evidence carefully, questioning witness testimony and knowing the difference between what might have happened and what has been proven.

Ralph Stanfield and 13 Co-accused on Trial in the Cape Town High Court

Cape Town High Court · 27 & 28 October 2026

Tuesday, 27 October 2026

The case against alleged gang boss Ralph Stanfield and 13 co-accused continued at the High Court on Tuesday, with further cross-examination of a witness. Stanfield, his wife Nicole Johnson and the co-accused face a wide range of charges, including the murder of gang leader Rashied Staggie in 2019 and of City of Cape Town employee Wendy Kloppers in 2023.

The prosecutor raised concerns about an alleged attempt by an attorney representing accused number six to contact a state witness, and asked the court to investigate. During the afternoon, video footage was played showing a tenant being assaulted by several accused. Questions were raised about the tenant's phone, and accused number one denied involvement in phone theft or fraud.

The defence also questioned Witness 3 about his duties as a supervisor, the security room and camera footage, challenging inconsistencies in his statements and his ability to recall events accurately.

Wednesday, 28 October 2026

The trial continued with the cross-examination of Witness 3. The defence challenged his credibility by questioning inconsistencies in his earlier testimony, including his claim that he went to Mr Dawie's office and whether he recorded the incident in the occurrence book. The witness maintained that he had made an entry and suggested the occurrence book be brought to court.

The defence also questioned an alleged statement that the group of accused were "untouchable", and whether accused number three had spoken to him about a tow truck that was coming. The witness maintained the conversation took place. The prosecutor returned to the video footage to clarify the pattern of the incident, including a conversation involving accused number two about an alleged R1 million theft. Several defence counsel objected as questions about the witness's credibility continued.

Safety and Security Campaign Launch

Nyanga, Cape Town · 21 October 2026

A Safety and Security Campaign was held in Nyanga on Wednesday, 21 October 2026, to address crime and strengthen preparations for the upcoming festive season. The campaign brought together government ministers, police officials, law enforcement agencies and community representatives.

During the campaign, speakers highlighted concerns about aggravated robbery, gender-based violence (GBV), extortion and other criminal activities. National and provincial leaders called on residents to cooperate with the police and other law enforcement agencies to prevent crime and protect vulnerable members of the community.

The campaign also included the presentation of an operational plan, demonstrations of police responses to a protest and a cash-in-transit heist, and the handover of new police vehicles. Officials emphasised the importance of effective policing, community partnerships and ensuring that officers have the resources needed to fight crime.

Speakers further encouraged the public to remain vigilant, obey the law and take responsibility for road and community safety. The campaign aims to strengthen cooperation between communities and law enforcement to create a safer Western Cape during the festive season.
