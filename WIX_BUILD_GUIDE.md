7# Building Enrique's portfolio in Wix (about 45 min)

The design is in `index.html`. Open it in Chrome to see the finished look. All text, captions and contact details are in that file, so copy them from there.

## Design (differs from the example)
- Colours: navy `#0f1b2d`, deeper navy `#16263d`, gold `#d9a441`, cream `#f6f1e7`, text `#1c2430`
- Fonts: **Playfair Display** (headings), **Montserrat** (body). Both are in Wix's font list.
- Set them under Site Design > Colours and Text.

## Steps
1. Wix > Create new site > blank template > Editor. Delete the placeholder content.
2. Upload every photo, video, the profile picture and the CV PDF to the Media Manager. Videos over ~1 GB may fail. Upload `EJ_VOX_POPS.mp4` (414 MB) to YouTube and use a Video > YouTube element instead.
3. Menu pages: Home, About, Portfolio, Gallery, Contact (or put everything on one page with anchors, as `index.html` does).
4. **Home:** a dark navy strip with the heading "Enrique Jason Claassen", the intro text, the profile picture on the right, and 3 gold buttons (View My Work, View My CV linked to the PDF, Contact Me).
5. **About:** the bio paragraphs plus the email, phone and LinkedIn (use the LinkedIn social icon, or a text link, pointing to https://www.linkedin.com/in/enrique-claassen-ba5a86383/).
6. **Portfolio:** one section per story (Taxi violence, Anele Tembe inquest, Ralph Stanfield trial, Nafiz Modack trial). Each has a headline, date line and text, then its videos below.
7. **Gallery:** Add > Image > Pro Gallery. Add the photos with the captions from `index.html`. Turn on the zoom/expand option.
8. **Contact:** Add > Contact > Contact Form, with fields Name, Email and Message. Set the form to send to enriqueclaassen65@gmail.com.
9. Check Mobile view, then Publish.

## Optional: use the HTML directly
You can paste the page into Add > Embed Code > Embed HTML, but images and videos must be hosted online, so it's not recommended. Building it natively in Wix is cleaner and what the lecturer will expect.
