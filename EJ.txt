POLICE BRIEFING (GREENPOINT POLICE STATION) ON TAXI VIOLENCE

A police briefing on taxi violence in the Western Cape is underway at the  Green Point Police Station. Provincial Police Commissioner Lieutenant General Sizakele Dyanti highlighted ongoing violence and shootings involving the taxi industry. Impact of Ongoing Taxi Violence on commuters


The ongoing taxi violence has affected operations at Cape Town International Airport, leaving commuters and airport staff facing transport challenges.

Some are experiencing delays and feared missing their flights, while others are facing long queues due to staff members being unable to get to work. Acting Regional Manager Malcolm Koopman apologised for the inconvenience and said that at least 50 percent of staff members had not reported for duty.

He said the airport was working with stakeholders to restore normal operations. Passengers were also urged to check their whatsapp messages, emails and other digital platforms for updates about their flights and procedures at the airport.
